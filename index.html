Everest Masala

Everest Masala is a heritage Indian spice brand established in 1947 with strong market recognition but an outdated visual identity — suffering from poor hierarchy, cluttered layouts, weak shelf presence, and difficult-to-use packaging.

Built to Speak

The rebrand is built around the idea that Everest blends are architecturally precise — carefully layered with intentional structure — and communicate culture, memory, and identity through flavor.

The mountain logo references both the brand name and the layered construction of spice blends. The teal-burgundy-gold palette provides modern shelf distinction rooted in Indian heritage.

Solving the UX

The redesign shifted from rigid boxes to resealable stand-up pouches, solving every UX pain point. A Mughal arch window acts as both functional element and visual metaphor.

Traditional Indian patterns function as visual "dialects" across different spice varieties — creating variety within a cohesive system.

Merchandise and Socials

The system extended across premium glass jars, branded merchandise, and a complete social media strategy for Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest — all built with Adobe Firefly across 100+ iterations.
